## Overall Impression

The page's central idea — the hero terminal demo — is close to perfect: it teaches the entire ladder concept in three lines of real designer vocabulary before the reader has scrolled once. ## Priority Issues

**[P1] Mobile nav has no wrap/collapse mechanism for 7 links + brand under 760px**
- **Why it matters**: This is the primary navigation on every page. `.nav-links{display:flex;gap:2px}` sets no `flex-wrap` and no hamburger fallback; the responsive rule only shrinks padding/font-size. Combined width at that size likely exceeds a 320–375px phone viewport, blocking the first interaction a mobile visitor has with the site. *(Computed from CSS, not rendered — verify on a real device.)*
- **Fix**: Collapse nav links to a menu button under 760px, or make `.nav-links` horizontally scrollable as a stopgap.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] The hero's "three lines" terminal demo likely wraps on mobile, undermining its own framing**
- **Why it matters**: `.term-bar` explicitly labels this "the whole idea, in three lines." Each line (~74 characters at 12.8px mono, including the trailing `→ you type it once`-style annotation) will exceed a phone-width `.term` box and wrap, breaking the exact promise the block makes. This is the page's stated peak moment.
- **Fix**: Hide or shorten the trailing `.out`/`.ok` annotations under a mobile breakpoint, the way `.rung .cmd` is already hidden at 760px.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] No trust reassurance at the `curl | bash` install moment for an explicitly skeptical audience**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md's audience "hasn't yet made AI a real part of how they work." `curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash` is a classic distrust-trigger pattern with zero on-page acknowledgment — no "this is Anthropic's official installer," no link to inspect the script first. This is the single highest-stakes action on the page, aimed at the persona most likely to hesitate at it.
- **Fix**: Add one sentence naming it as the official installer, with a link to inspect the script before running.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] "Where to go next" 7-card wall violates chunking/minimal-choices and weakens the ending**
- **Why it matters**: 7 equally-weighted cards with no visual differentiation beyond a tiny 10.5px low-contrast "Read first" label create decision paralysis at the page's closing beat — exactly where peak-end theory says a strong singular impression should land instead. Casey (mobile, scrolling fast) has roughly 1-in-7 odds of landing on the intended starting point.
- **Fix**: Visually promote Foundations (larger card or a real primary-button CTA above the grid: "Start with Foundations →"), demote the remaining 6 into a visually secondary row.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Real WCAG AA contrast failures using the design system's own stated hex values**
- **Why it matters**: `--ink-3 (#86868F)` on `--paper (#E7E6E1)` computes to ≈2.9:1 — used for the hero eyebrow (the very first text on the page) and the entire footer. `--ink-3` on `--card (#FCFBF8)` (the `.rung .cmd` hints) computes to ≈3.5:1. Both fall short of WCAG AA's 4.5:1 for normal text, on a page whose whole job is building trust with a skeptical reader.
- **Fix**: Darken `--ink-3` usage on Paper backgrounds, or restrict `--ink-3` to card-surface contexts where contrast is closer to compliant.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`
