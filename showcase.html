FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are ingesting data within ten minutes using the one-line agent install, and have their first tuned alerts the same afternoon.

Do you have a free plan?

Yes — Starter is free forever for up to three services, with seven days of history and email alerts. No card required.

Can I bring my existing tools?

Acme integrates with the common metrics, logging and tracing sources, and exposes an API so you can pipe in anything custom.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export everything at any time. After cancellation we retain data for 30 days so you can come back, then it's permanently deleted.
